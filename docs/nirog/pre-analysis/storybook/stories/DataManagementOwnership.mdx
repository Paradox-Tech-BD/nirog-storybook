# Charter and Domain Ownership

The data-management charter requires a declared purpose, accountable owner, classification, authoritative location, lifecycle, and recovery behavior for every persistent data product. Logical PostgreSQL schemas enforce the ownership model: a module owns writes to its business tables, while other modules use commands, versioned events, or authorized read models.

<MermaidDiagram title="Module ownership and permitted data dependencies" chart={ownershipMap} />

The Platform module owns cross-cutting controls, not medication decisions. Its retention worker calls an owning module’s purge service; its outbox records committed effects; its audit records redacted decision evidence. It does not bypass domain ownership with arbitrary SQL.
