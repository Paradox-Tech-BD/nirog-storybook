# Medication Regimen and Adherence

The regimen subsystem owns the confirmed medication course. It is distinct from a catalog product and from OCR evidence. A regimen connects a patient profile to either an approved product or a private unresolved medication. Its medication-critical instructions are versioned so that historical dose events stay meaningful after an edit.

## Important rule

A reminder delivery is not evidence of medication consumption. `Notification` records that a reminder was scheduled, sent, acknowledged, snoozed, or expired. `DoseLog` records what the user reported: taken, skipped, late, or a system-inferred missed state. Adherence statistics are projections derived from dose logs, not a replacement for them.
