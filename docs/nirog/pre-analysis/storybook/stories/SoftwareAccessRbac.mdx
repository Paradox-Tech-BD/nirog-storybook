# RBAC Roles, Permissions, and Grants

Nirog implements **RBAC now** through a versioned permission registry and scoped profile grants. Role templates are administrative convenience definitions. When a caregiver grant is created, the template expands into a persisted permission snapshot in `identity.profile_access.permission_set`. Runtime authorization evaluates that snapshot—not the live template—so a later template change cannot silently broaden an old grant.

<MermaidDiagram title="Role template to profile capability" chart={rbacGrantModel} />

An action permission is necessary but never sufficient. Every profile-data decision also proves the actor’s current owner/caregiver relationship, the target resource’s profile scope, applicable consent/purpose, lifecycle state, and the module’s business validation. Team membership remains an invitation/collaboration association, not health-data authority.

**Profile owner** has the full owner workflow through the implicit owner relationship, but never catalog/platform rights or bypass of consent/state checks. **Caregiver viewer** can read profile, regimen, adherence, and evidence metadata, but cannot access raw images, mutate health state, manage devices, or share. **Caregiver participant** adds document contribution and purpose-gated image access, but not regimen/adherence mutation or sharing. **Caregiver manager** adds regimen, adherence, and notification actions, but not ownership transfer, grant delegation, or catalog/platform access.
