# Evidence and Medication Drafts

The evidence subsystem converts a user-supplied prescription file into a **bounded, traceable, editable medication-draft workflow**. It is designed to automate extraction while preserving a clear clinical-commit boundary.

<MermaidDiagram title="Evidence and draft state progression" chart={lifecycle} />

## Secure evidence intake

The user asks Core to authorize a supported, size-bounded upload for a selected prescription. Core creates the evidence metadata and returns a short-lived upload capability. The browser transfers the object directly to private evidence storage. The evidence remains incomplete until the authenticated completion command succeeds.

Completion is the transactional enqueue point. Core moves evidence into processing and commits the OCR job, audit information, and identifier-only outbox event together. The outbox payload contains references required for delivery, not raw evidence, OCR text, or medication data.

## Extraction provenance

An OCR result has a source classification and may include model and pipeline metadata. This allows product surfaces and authorized caregivers to distinguish the provenance of a persisted extraction without exposing raw provider exchanges. An extraction is an evidence-derived artifact; it is not a medication regimen.

The workflow records success, retryable failure, permanent failure, and terminal states through Core. Retryable processing conditions can schedule future work. Permanent or exhausted conditions leave evidence in a safe non-success outcome rather than claiming a draft exists.

## Medication draft contract

Each extracted medication candidate can produce a profile-scoped draft. The draft carries editable fields such as medication name, dose, route, frequency, schedule, and interval, along with field-level confidence. Candidate count, extraction provenance, and draft status support an understandable review surface without requiring a human gate before extraction.

The product threshold is **0.70**. At or above the threshold, a qualifying field can auto-populate. Below the threshold—or when missing—the field remains visibly editable and should be marked for correction. The threshold is a UI assistance rule, not a substitute for clinical validation or consent.

<MermaidDiagram title="Automatic extraction and explicit clinical submission boundary" chart={draftBoundary} />

## Submission is always explicit

The automatic path ends after extraction and draft persistence. A patient or authorized caregiver must explicitly submit a validated regimen command. Core confirms current permission, validates all required regimen fields, creates the regimen, and records the draft’s submitted linkage transactionally.

> No worker, dispatcher, upload completion endpoint, or confidence threshold may create a regimen.

Caregivers can inspect authorized evidence provenance, draft state, confidence, corrections, and audit metadata. Their visibility does not make extraction contingent on a routine caregiver approval gate. Default caregiver access does not include regimen-write authority.

## Safety and retention expectations

Evidence access is lease-bound and time-limited. Raw OCR text and provider payloads are not suitable for outbox, audit, or user-facing operational logs. Retention, deletion, and export behavior must follow the evidence record and profile policy rather than convenience copies made by downstream subsystems.
