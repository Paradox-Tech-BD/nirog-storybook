# System Context and Quality Attributes

Nirog is a medication-management system, not an EHR, prescribing engine, or diagnosis service. It lets permitted people preserve evidence, review assisted extraction, explicitly create/manage a personal regimen, report dose behavior, and receive advisory reminder/refill support. The backend—not the client or provider—holds final authority over profile scope, medication state, and retention.

<MermaidDiagram title="System actors, external boundaries, and Nirog trust boundary" chart={contextMap} />

The architecture prioritizes medication safety, confidentiality, explainability, retry correctness, and controlled change. It degrades to manual entry or a clear pending/failure state instead of transforming model/provider uncertainty into a medication action.
